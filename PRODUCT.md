# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who live in two or more languages: expats, language learners, remote workers on international teams.
They are mid-task on a Mac (writing an email, reading an article, chatting) when they hit a word they need.
The job: get the translation in under two seconds without leaving the current app, then get back to work.

## Product Purpose

Reversobar is a free, open-source macOS menu-bar translator powered by Reverso.
Press Option-Space anywhere, type, get instant translations across 18 languages, copy/hear/save, done.
The site exists to get the ZIP downloaded and to make a tiny utility feel like a considered product.
Success: a visitor understands the product in one fold and clicks Download.

## Brand Personality

Wayfinding, decisive, utilitarian.
The brand is transit signage for language: the visual language of metro and airport wayfinding - flat saturated color fields, bold grotesque caps, arrows as grammar.
It should feel like a sign you trust at a glance, not a SaaS landing page that wants a demo call.

## Anti-references

- The 2025 AI-generated landing page: indigo/violet mesh-gradient glow, glassmorphism cards, gradient text, dark-first "dev-tool aesthetic". This is what the first version looked like and it was explicitly rejected.
- Raycast/Linear-style bento grids of identical glowing cards.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display serif italics, drop caps) - wrong register for a utility.

## Design Principles

1. Signage, not decoration: every visual element should work like wayfinding - flat color, high contrast, instant read. If it only exists to shimmer, cut it.
2. Show the product early: the real popover screenshot appears in the first fold, unretouched.
3. Arrows are the grammar: direction (EN → RU, privet → привет, download ↓) is the brand's native gesture; use it instead of icons-for-everything.
4. Drenched commitment: the signal-blue field IS the site. No hedging into neutral gray sections.
5. One fold, one idea: long scroll, deliberate pacing, a single dominant statement per section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 against its field (white on signal blue must stay above it), large display ≥3:1.
Full prefers-reduced-motion support: WebGL and marquee effects fall back to static equivalents.
Mixed Latin/Cyrillic display type must use a face with native Cyrillic (no tofu, no faux-italic transliteration).
